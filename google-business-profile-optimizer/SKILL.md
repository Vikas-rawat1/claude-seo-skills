# Google Business Profile Optimization Intelligence

## PURPOSE

Analyze a specific business and create a business-specific Google Business Profile optimization strategy.

The objective is not to provide a generic GBP checklist.

The objective is:

> Understand the actual business → understand the actual location → understand the actual services/products → understand local search demand → analyze competitors → identify gaps → improve relevance, distance, and prominence → create an implementation-ready GBP plan.

Every recommendation must be specific to the business being analyzed.

---

# 1. GOOGLE'S LOCAL RANKING FRAMEWORK

Use Google's official terminology:

### Relevance

How well the Business Profile matches what someone is searching for.

### Distance

How far the business is from the searcher or the location implied by the search.

### Prominence

How well-known the business is.

Google states that local results are mainly based on:

> Relevance + Distance + Prominence

Do not claim to know Google's exact weighting of these factors.

Do not invent percentages.

Do not claim that one factor always outweighs another.

---

# 2. PROXIMITY TERMINOLOGY

The user may refer to this factor as:

**Distance / Proximity**
